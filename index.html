How to play

Select one ball and choose a direction. It keeps sliding until it hits a wall, a boundary, or another ball. Dock every ball on a matching goal.
◎

Stop to dock

A matching goal locks a ball only when it stops there. Docked balls stay put and block other balls.

↱

Follow the arrows

Arrows redirect a slide as you enter. At a red rotating arrow, choose its exit direction; this represents waiting for that direction.

◉

Stop on a switch

Stop on a switch to toggle it red/green and change its linked walls or teleports. A wall group stays unchanged if turning it on would trap a ball inside a wall.

◌

Use a teleport

Colored teleports accept matching balls; gray universal teleports accept any color. Both preserve direction. If an exit is occupied, the ball stops at the entrance. Inactive teleports are dim.

⬡

One-use hexagons

Green subtracts; red adds. Passing through consumes a hexagon for every ball. One slide always adds one to the move score.

↶

History navigation

Ctrl+Z deletes the last move, only at the end of the route. Navigate without deleting: Shift+Z or Home = Start; Z or PgUp = Previous; X or PgDn = Next; Shift+X or End = End.

Solution notation

Rur,G3ld,Bu means red up/right, green 3 left/down, blue up. Numbering is fixed from the starting board, left to right and bottom to top. Omit the number when only one movable ball of that color remains. Ru(->l)d chooses left at a rotating arrow during the upward slide, then moves down.

Board format & coordinates

Coordinates are 1-based, with (1, 1) at the bottom left. Use colors R, G, B, Y and directions u, d, l, r. The grid is rectangular; list blocked cells in walls. Optional mechanic arrays may be omitted.

{
  "version": 1, "id": "my-board", "title": "My board",
  "width": 5, "height": 4,
  "balls": [{"color":"R","x":1,"y":1}],
  "goals": [{"color":"R","x":5,"y":4}],
  "walls": [{"x":3,"y":2}],
  "arrows": [{"x":3,"y":1,"direction":"u","rotating":true}],
  "solution": "Rr(->r)u"
}

gates: {id, group, x, y, active}. switches: {id, x, y, wallGroups: [group], teleportIds: [id], on?}; optional on: true starts the switch green. teleports: {id, color, ends: [{x,y},{x,y}], active}; use color: "*" for a universal pair, or R/G/B/Y for a matching-color pair. hexes: {id, x, y, value}. Negative hex values subtract from the score.
